## Affine Transformations 

Affine transformations are linear transformation that are followed with a translation directly after. Affine transformations are a superset of linear transformation meaning that 

All linear transformations is an affine translation, but not all affine transformations are linear transformations 

Which also means that 

Any transformation that is represented in the form

$A(\vec{x}) = T\vec{x} + \vec{b}$

is an affine transformation as now the object is off-centered from the origin


## Angle Preserving Transformations 

Angle preserving transformations are transformation that once performed preserve the original angles of the object after the transformation.

These transformations include 
- Translation
- Rotation
- Uniform Scale


## Orthogonal Transformations

Orthogonal Transformations where the transformation matrix is orthonormal. Only Translation, Rotation and Reflection are orthogonal transformations where the **magnitudes** of their lengths, area, volume and angles are preserved 

## Rigid body Transformations

Rigid body transformations are transformations that change the location and orientation of an object but **does not** manipulate the shape.

This means only translation and rotation transformations are rigid body transformations preserving angles, lengths, areas and volumes
