These operations can be combined into one whole equation.

$\begin{aligned} \vec{a} = (3, -6) \\[1em] \vec{b} = (-2, 5) \\[1em] 2\vec{b} + {1 \over 3}\vec{a} = \vec{c} \\[2em] \vec{c} = (-4 + 3,\; 10 + (-2)) \\[1em] \vec{c} = (-1, 8)\end{aligned}$
