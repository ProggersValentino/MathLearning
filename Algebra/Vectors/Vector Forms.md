| Form                                                                                                                                  | Meaning               | Example                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------ |

| [Unit Vectors](obsidian://open?vault=MathLearning&file=Algebra%2FVectors%2FUnit%20Vectors)                                            | $a\hat{i} + b\hat{j}$ | $4 \cdot 1 + 5 \cdot 1$  |
| [Magnitude](obsidian://open?vault=MathLearning&file=Algebra%2FVectors%2FMagnitude)                                                    | $\|\|\vec{m}\|\|$     | $\|\|\vec{m}\|\| = 7.43$ |
| [Direction](obsidian://open?vault=MathLearning&file=Algebra%2FVectors%2FVectors%20to%20Direction)                                     | $\theta$              | $\theta = 65.12^{\circ}$ |
